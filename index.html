Project Architecture

  • Business Analyst Lifecycle: Authored BRDs and FRDs aligning KPIs (turnover ratio, sell-through).
  • Data Engineering: Engineered SQL CTEs and Window Functions for rolling 7-day and 30-day demand.
  • Predictive Modeling: ARIMA and Facebook Prophet for seasonality and promotional volatility.
  • Power BI Integration: Interactive DAX calculations and executive drill-throughs.
